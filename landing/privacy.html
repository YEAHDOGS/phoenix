Privacy policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · A simple baseline policy, to be expanded as Phoenix grows.

The short version

This landing page collects nothing. No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no ads.

The notify form

The email form on the home page is a front-end placeholder and is not yet connected to any backend. Nothing you type is transmitted or stored.

Fonts and hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare and loads no third-party resources: system typefaces only, no external scripts, no fonts. Like most infrastructure providers, they may log basic technical request data (such as IP addresses) to operate their services — see the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Changes

When Phoenix launches for real, this policy will be replaced with a complete one before any data is ever collected.
